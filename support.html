Support — TattingElf

An offline tatting pattern design tool for iPad.

Contact

Need help or found a bug? Email aileen_w_vision@126.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Requirements

iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. An Apple Pencil is recommended for drawing.

Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

TattingElf is free to download. You can try all features free for 7 days. After the trial ends, you can still open and view your work, but editing and exporting require the full version. The trial can be started once per Apple ID.

How do I buy the full version or upgrade?

Open the purchase screen in the App. You can buy the Full Version (one-time, yours forever) or, if you already own an earlier version, an Upgrade. There is no subscription.

I bought it before — how do I restore my purchase?

Open the purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I move my work to a new iPad or keep devices in sync?

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud Drive is on, your projects and material library sync automatically through your private iCloud. Just sign in with the same Apple ID on the other device. You can also back up your whole device (iCloud Backup or computer backup) or export/import files manually.

What is a “conflict copy”?

If the same project is edited on two devices at the same time, the App keeps both versions. The extra one is saved alongside with a name like “MyPattern (conflict copy 2026-10-14 15-30)” so nothing is lost.

Where are my files stored?

On your iPad, and (if iCloud is on) in your private iCloud. The developer cannot access your files. See the Privacy Policy.

Refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple. For refunds, use Apple's “Report a Problem” page.
