Privacy Policy — TattingElf

Last updated: October 14, 2026

TattingElf (“the App”) is an offline tatting pattern design tool for iPad. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. We do not collect your personal data

The developer of TattingElf does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The App contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts, and does not use third-party SDKs that collect data.

2. Where your content is stored

  • On your device. The patterns, projects, material library, and other content you create are stored locally in the App's sandbox on your iPad.
  • In your private iCloud (optional). If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud Drive is enabled, the App uses iCloud Documents to sync your projects and material library across your own devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud account. The developer has no access to it.

3. Photos and files you import

If you import an image or file as a reference, it is processed on your device. It is not uploaded to the developer. If iCloud is enabled, such imported content may be included in the private iCloud sync described above.

4. Purchases

Purchases and downloads are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The App is a general-purpose design tool and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

6. Data retention and deletion

You control your content. You can delete projects and materials within the App at any time; deleting them removes them from your device (and, if enabled, from your iCloud). Uninstalling the App removes its local data from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
